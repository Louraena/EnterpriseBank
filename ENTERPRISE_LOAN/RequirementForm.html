Enterprise Loan Requirements

(Before you click the link to our Facebook page, please make sure you have all your requirements ready to send. Once you’re all set, you can easily submit them through our Facebook page.)

  • Bank statement (at least 6 months)
  • Sales record (at least 6 months)
  • In-house financial statement with 2 years comparative
  • ITR & AFS for 3 years duly stamped by BIR
  • Photocopy of sales receipts/purchases for the last 3 months
  • List of 3 major suppliers with name and contact number
  • Aging of receivables / list of receivables
  • List of loans as of December 2025
  • TIN or TIP verification
  • Valid IDs with signature (3 specimen)
  • 2x2 picture (2 copies)
  • Marriage certificate
  • Updated business permit
  • DTI certificate
  • Picture of business inventories (if applicable)
  • List of property, plant & equipment
  • Bills of materials, building plans, & cost estimates
  • Company profile & ownership structure (for corporation)
  • Bio-data or management credentials of major stakeholders & key officers (for corporation)
  • General Information Sheet (GIS) (for corporation)
  • SEC registration
  • Articles of Incorporation / Partnership and By-laws

Collaterals

  • REM (Real Estate Mortgage):
  • CTC of title
  • Title copy
  • Sketch map
  • Tax receipt (updated)
  • Tax declaration (updated)
  • Tax clearance (updated)
  • Chattel Mortgage:
  • LTO certificate with encumbrance
  • MVMR & MVIR
  • Chattel picture
  • OR/CR - Original
